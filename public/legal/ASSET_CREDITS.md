# Asset credits and provenance

Only final runtime assets are shipped. Starter/rejected models, reference sheets and authoring renders are excluded.

## Project models and interface

The 17 GLBs in `public/assets/models/` are original geometry produced for this project with Codex, guided by the supplied chess-character and mansion references. They include seven characters, an alternate King pose, six furnished rooms and three ending props. The supplied starter GLBs are not loaded. The asset manifest lists their mesh and triangle counts.

Runtime architecture is generated by `src/world.ts`; the interface, notebook, floor-plan controls, chessboard and evidence icons are original TypeScript/CSS/SVG. Editable Blender sources and the reproducible authoring script are retained locally rather than included in this deployment repository. No third-party CC licence is asserted for the original project work.

## Supplied narrative, audio and wax seal

The story/layout JSON, eleven synthesized WAVs and wax-seal SVG originate in the user-supplied CHECKMATE AT MIDNIGHT handoff. The audio uses additive/noise synthesis rather than sampled recordings. The original permission to use, modify and redistribute these project assets is preserved in `public/legal/HANDOFF-CREDITS.md`. Reference images were used for direction and are not distributed as game assets.

## Historical chess position

The optional Study chessboard shows Kasparov–Topalov, Wijk aan Zee 1999, around 24.Rxd4 and 25.Re7+. Position facts were checked against https://en.chessbase.com/newsroom/post/throwback-thursday-kasparov-wijjk-1999. The SVG board and system chess glyph rendering are original. No article images, annotations or JavaScript are bundled.

Runtime file hashes are in `public/legal/ASSET_HASHES.json`. No paid assets, ambiguous model reuploads or downloaded executable scripts are included.
